About ICRA Satellite School

World-Class Education in Robotics

The global ICRA spirit, hosted in Tunisia

The IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation is one of the world’s leading robotics and automation conferences, gathering researchers, industry leaders, and academics to share advances in robotics, AI, intelligent systems, and automation.

The ICRA Satellite School is an intensive educational program organized by the IEEE RAS Tunisia Chapter in collaboration with IEEE RAS. It brings together leading minds in robotics to share cutting-edge research and practical knowledge.

Whether you are a student, researcher, or industry professional, this program offers a unique opportunity to accelerate your career in robotics and automation.
